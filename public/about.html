Sync AI is built and maintained by the Aroxiania AI team — a group of builders passionate about making meaningful connections easier with the power of AI. We're based in India and building for India.

Technology

Sync AI is powered by state-of-the-art large language models via Hugging Face, built with React + Vite, authenticated securely through Firebase, and designed mobile-first for modern users.
